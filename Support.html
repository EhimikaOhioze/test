Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my account balance?

You can view your available balance from your PrimeTrust Bank account dashboard.

How do I view my transactions?

Visit the Transactions page to view your recent account transactions.

How do I transfer money?

Select the Transfer Money option from your banking dashboard and provide the required recipient and transaction information.

What should I do if I notice an unfamiliar transaction?

Contact PrimeTrust Bank customer support immediately so the transaction can be reviewed.

How do I update my personal information?

Visit your customer profile and select the option to update your information.
