Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25 September 2026

PrimeTrust Bank respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our banking services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our services, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Residential address
  • Identification information
  • Account information
  • Transaction information

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected may be used to:

  • Provide banking services
  • Manage customer accounts
  • Process transactions
  • Communicate with customers
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve our services
  • Protect customer accounts

3. Information Security

PrimeTrust Bank takes reasonable measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

4. Sharing of Information

Customer information is treated as confidential. Information may only be shared where necessary to provide services, comply with applicable requirements, protect customers or protect the security of the bank.

5. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials, passwords and other security information confidential.

Contact PrimeTrust Bank immediately if you believe your account information has been compromised.

6. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use technologies such as cookies or similar tools to improve website functionality and user experience.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

PrimeTrust Bank may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be made available through our website.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team.
